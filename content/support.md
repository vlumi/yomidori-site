Need a hand, found a bug, or have an idea? Here's how to reach it.

## Report a bug or request a feature

Yomidori is developed in the open. The best place for bugs and requests is the
issue tracker:

- [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/vlumi/yomidori/issues)

Please say which device and OS version, and what you did — a photo of the page
that went wrong helps most of all. Email works too: <ville@misaki.fi>.

Yomidori is free with no ads or in-app purchases. If you'd like to support the
work, you can [sponsor the developer on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/vlumi)
— entirely optional.

## Common questions

**Where are my cards?**
On your device, and in your own iCloud while *Sync with iCloud* is on in
Settings (it is, unless you turned it off), so your iPhone, iPad and Mac show
the same cards. Nothing is kept anywhere else. Settings also saves a backup
file of the cards, the collections and the lookup history, which restores on
any device.

**Is it one app for iPhone, iPad and Mac?**
Yes — one app under one purchase, with the same cards on all of them.

**The reading is wrong, or the word was cut in the wrong place.**
Text read off paper is sometimes read wrong by a character, and a word joined
from several pieces can be cut either way. Under a word, *Fix the text* corrects
a character and reads the page again; a joined word also lists the words it is
made of, each to keep by itself. If the dictionary itself is wrong, that's
worth an issue.

**It reads the page sideways, or doesn't read it at all.**
The page is read the way the phone was held when you pressed *Read*; if the
phone was flat or turned, turn it upright and read again. Vertical text, as in
a paperback, is read as columns; the *Vision* and *Close-up* recognizers under
the page are there for a line Live Text misses.

**What are the birds?**
How far a card has come: an egg is waiting for its lesson, then a hatchling,
chick, fledgling, flying and migrating bird as the word stays remembered
longer. A word you forget goes down a step, not back to the egg, and a word
you truly forgot can be sent back to waiting from the review.

**How often does a word come back?**
On the FSRS schedule, aimed at remembering 90 % of what comes up. *Aim to
remember* in Settings makes it 95 %, which brings each word back about twice
as often.

**I changed the language and nothing happened.**
The language setting takes effect the next time you open the app — the system
loads it at launch. Quit and reopen Yomidori and it'll be in the new language.

**Is there any tracking or ads?**
No. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. See the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
